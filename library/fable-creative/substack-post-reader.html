The case for cooking one thing badly, every week

On the quiet competence that comes from repetition, and why your first risotto should be terrible.

JM
June MarlowOct 22, 2026 · 7 min read
2483619

There is a particular kind of dinner that nobody photographs. It is a Tuesday. The onions caught slightly. The rice is a shade past where it should be and you have, once again, under-salted the stock. You eat it anyway, standing up, and it is fine, and that is the whole point.

Most writing about cooking is about outcomes. This is a piece about reps. I want to argue that the single most useful thing a home cook can do is to pick one dish, make it every week for a year, and allow it to be bad for the first ten.
