#Design principles, v3

#design/principles#lumen

We write these down so that decisions survive the people who made them. *Three* principles carry most of the weight; everything else is commentary.

##1. Clarity over cleverness

If a screen needs a tooltip to explain its primary action, **the screen is not finished yet**. Counter-example: our old export dialog, where ⌘E did three different things depending on focus.

"Simple is not the same as easy. Simple is what is left when you remove everything that is not the point." — from the offsite notes

##2. The content is the brightest thing

Chrome recedes, content advances. That is why the sidebar is dark and ::the note is the only white surface:: in the window.
