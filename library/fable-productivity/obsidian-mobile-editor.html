We write these down so decisions survive the people who made them. Context lives in Offsite notes and the 2026-10-18 daily note.

1. Clarity over cleverness

If a screen needs a tooltip to explain its primary action, the screen is not finished. See Billing v4 crit for the counter-example with ⌘E.

Note
Simple is what is left when you remove everything that is not the point.

2. Content is the brightest thing

Chrome recedes, content advances. Related: Why the sidebar is dark #design/type

  • Test on the dark theme first
  • Hairlines, never shadows, inside the editor
