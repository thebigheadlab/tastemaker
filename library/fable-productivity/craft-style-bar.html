Design principles

We write these down so that decisions survive the people who made them. Three principles carry most of the weight.

⋮⋮Clarity over cleverness. If a screen needs a tooltip to explain its primary action, the screen is not finished yet.

Every principle below comes with a counter-example from our own product, because that is the only kind of example anyone remembers.
